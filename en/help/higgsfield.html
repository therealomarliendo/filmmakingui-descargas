HiggsfieldUI

Direct access to Higgsfield's image and video models (Seedance, Kling O3, Kling 3.0…) with all their parameters. It needs the Higgsfield key (Key ID and Secret) in Settings.

  1. Choose the family and the model.
  2. Fill in the model's fields; for references you can use the library, a gallery or generate an image.
  3. Cost: Higgsfield does not publish prices. The app estimates them from real generations and says so («measured» or «estimated»). With Record real cost you note what your Higgsfield dashboard shows and improve the estimates.
