Transform video

Four modes, each for something different:

  • Motion control: animates a frame with the motion of a video (characters and background come from the image; the motion, from the video).
  • Animate character: applies a video's motion to a character.
  • Replace character: swaps the person in a video for your character, keeping everything else.

How to use it

  1. Video: the motion video or the video to edit.
  2. Reference image: a character, an uploaded image, one from a gallery or a generated one.
  3. Mode options: model, resolution, prompt, improve identity…
  4. Generate: the button shows the cost. Below you have the history.

It needs ffmpeg: it comes with the Windows installer; in the portable version, open «Instalar ffmpeg.bat»; on Mac, brew install ffmpeg.
