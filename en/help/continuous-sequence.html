Sequence

Long videos that look like a single take, segment by segment.

Modes

  • By keyframes (recommended): first the final image of each segment is generated and you approve it; then each video goes from its start image to its end image (first-and-last-frame models). It avoids accumulated drift.
  • Chained by last frame: each segment starts on the last frame of the previous one. «Exact join» starts right on that frame; «reference models» use it as a guide.

Create a sequence

  1. First frame: upload it, choose it from a gallery or generate a new one.
  2. Video model and, in keyframe mode, the keyframes' image model (GPT Image 2 medium by default).
  3. Reference characters and, with two or more, their position (left, center, right, background) so they do not swap.
  4. Prompts: one line per segment, with a single action (in English). You can ask the AI to suggest them.
  5. The cost is shown in two parts (keyframes and videos). Create does not generate anything.

Characters per segment

In each segment, «Change cast» lets someone enter (from the left, the right, the background, behind the camera or «already present», with their final position) or exit. The cast carries over to the next segment. In chained mode, only reference models accept new characters (the app warns you and suggests one).

Review and join

  • Approve each keyframe or regenerate it (the following ones become out of date; nothing is deleted).
  • Continue generates the next segment; each one is approved or regenerated (previous takes are kept).
  • Regenerate with… tries another model for that segment only.
  • If a possible character swap or a camera cut is detected, the app flags it and never approves it on its own.
  • When all are approved, Join creates a single video (with an optional smooth join).
