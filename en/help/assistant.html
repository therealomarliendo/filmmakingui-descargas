Assistant

The app as a chat: tell it what you want to create (an explainer, a short film with dialogue, a tutorial, an ad…) and it drives the sections for you. It is the home screen; the Assistant | Studio switch at the top takes you to the usual interface.

How it works

  1. You ask for what you want. If something essential is missing it asks *one* question with quick answers.
  2. It proposes a plan: ordered steps, the section of each, the model, the cost of each step, the total cost and the estimated duration. It spends nothing until you press Approve and run.
  3. It runs real jobs (you also see them in the jobs list). Progress and thumbnails appear in the chat.
  4. It reviews images and frames before delivering; if something fails it proposes redoing only that (with its cost).
  5. It delivers with a player, Download, Share and Open in Studio. Everything created stays in its normal sections.

Spending control

  • Nothing paid runs without an approved plan. A step outside the plan, or over it, asks for its own additional approval.
  • Cap per conversation (default $5): it also counts what the chat itself spends. Change it at the top, next to the spend.
  • Saving a project's JSON only happens if you approve it in the plan (with a .bak copy).
  • The global safety switch still rules.

Correct on the fly

Write at any time: “change scene 3”, “shorter”, “another voice”. It handles your message as soon as the current step ends and, if there is a new cost, asks you to approve it. Stop halts the work (what was already launched stays in the jobs list).

Attachments

With Attach you can upload images, audio, video or scripts (txt, md, srt, json…). It can use a photo as a character or location, an audio as a voice (to clone it you must confirm it is yours or you have permission) or material, and a text as a script.

Conversations

They are saved per project and can be resumed whenever you want, even after restarting the app (if it was working, Resume appears).

What it needs

Your Anthropic API key (Settings). Model: Claude Sonnet 5.5 by default; tick *Plan with Opus* to understand and plan with Claude Opus (execution continues with Sonnet). The Assistant uses the same tools as the MCP server (Settings → Local MCP server).
