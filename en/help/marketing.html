Marketing

Product images and videos ready for ads and social media.

Categories

  • Product Shot: product image (studio backdrop, in hand, with natural elements, on the street, splashes…).
  • Motion: animates the product.
  • UGC: portrait video with a person presenting the product.

How to use it

  1. Choose the category and the preset (the kind of shot, with its prompt ready).
  2. Choose the product: one registered in Props, a photo, an image from a gallery or a new image.
  3. Characters: optional in Product Shot and required in some UGC presets (you can add several).
  4. Check the model and its estimated cost and generate.

From a Product Shot result you can click Animate to go straight to Motion.
