Film

Film or series projects: scenes, shots and takes with consistent characters and places.

Project

  • + New project: name, live action or animation (for animation, a description and 1 to 3 style images that apply to everything) and orientation, landscape 16:9 or portrait 9:16.
  • No project: to try a loose image; it goes to the loose shots gallery.
  • The spending chip shows what the project has spent and the number of takes.

Three ways to create

  • Single shot: one shot from an action prompt (in English), with characters, location, props, lens and image model.
  • Script to Storyboard: paste a script and the AI turns it into scenes and shots. Before generating you can review and edit everything; characters, places or props that are not in your library are resolved in a panel (use an existing one, create it or ignore it).
  • JSON (advanced): write or paste the storyboard as JSON. «Copy prompt for your AI» prepares a text for ChatGPT or Claude with the format, the rules and your library's names. «Full documentation» opens the reference for every field.

Gallery

The project's scenes and shots. On each card:

  • Regenerate: a new take (previous ones are kept).
  • Edit: change that shot's prompt.
  • Animate: generate a video from the chosen take, with its motion prompt.
  • View the prompt sent, Share and Report.

Tips

  • One shot = one frozen moment; one animation = a single action. Two actions are two shots.
  • Shots without people (landscapes, objects) should have no characters.
  • With GPT Image the cost goes up with each reference image (characters, location, props).
