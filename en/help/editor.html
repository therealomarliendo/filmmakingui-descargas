Editor

FilmmakingUI's video editor: join your shots on a timeline, add effects, transitions and text, and export the final video. It costs no credit: everything runs on your computer.

Don't confuse it with Transform video (under TOOLS), which transforms a video with AI (motion control, replace character, lip-sync…). The Editor is the cutting room; Transform video is a special effect.

Where it is

  • Sidebar → PRODUCTION → Editor.
  • The Montage tab in Film (the same editor, with Film's project).
  • Send to montage button on any video in any section: it adds it to the montage you choose.

How to use it

  1. Pick the project and a montage (or create a new one). It saves itself.
  2. In the left panel, the Gallery tab shows everything you have generated across all projects and sections. Drag a clip onto the timeline or press +.
  3. Trim by dragging the edges, split with S, step frame by frame with the arrow keys and zoom the timeline in to the frame.
  4. Effects and Transitions (left panel): vignette, grain, motion blur, glitch, whip pan, flash, slow motion, speed ramps, cinematic bars and color filters. They show instantly in the preview.
  5. Export (top right): creates the final video. Each export is a new version and appears below with Share and Download.

AI editor

Below the editor. Pick a style (vlog, trailer, action, social media…), tell Claude what you want and it builds or changes the montage. Before sending you see the estimated cost; each montage has a spending limit that saves when you change it.

  • Recommended model: Claude Sonnet 5.5 (default). Claude Haiku 5.5 is cheaper but edits worse.
  • The result is applied to the editor and you can undo it with Ctrl+Z inside the editor.

Montages from earlier versions

Montages from 1.7 and 1.8 appear in the list as «version 1.7». Opening one makes a copy in the new editor (clips, trims, volume, fades, zoom, text and audio); the original is not touched.
