Recorder

Record video tutorials: your screen, your voice and, if you like, your face; the app composes the video in horizontal or vertical, marks zooms and adds subtitles. Recording and composing cost nothing.

Where it is

Sidebar → PRODUCTION → Recorder.

How to use it

  1. Pick microphone, camera and, if your browser allows it, system audio. Press Start recording and choose which screen or window to share.
  2. While recording, press Z (or Mark zoom) at key moments. If your browser offers it, a floating window shows the buttons so you don't have to come back to the tab.
  3. Stop: the recording is saved in your app.
  4. Composition: choose *screen only*, *bubble* (your face in a circle), *side by side* or *AI avatar in a bubble*, the 16:9 or 9:16 format and the position. Compose creates a new version every time.
  5. Subtitles (paid, charged per minute of voice and cached so it isn't repeated): tick the box and press Generate subtitles.
  6. Assemble in the editor (free): creates a montage with the latest version, a gentle zoom at every mark and the subtitles; there you add music and titles and export.

AI avatar (optional, paid)

Your photo «speaks» with your own recorded voice. You may only use your own face or the face of a person who gave you express permission; never celebrities or third parties. The composed video carries the label «Avatar generado con IA» / AI-generated avatar. It is charged per second of audio according to the model (the cost is shown first).

Notes

  • Files are stored in your outputs (_grabaciones folder) and the originals are never touched.
  • Every composed video is H.264, compatible with phones and WhatsApp.
