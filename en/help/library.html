Library: characters, locations and props

Reusable elements so a character, a place or an object looks the same in every shot.

Create

  • + New and choose how: cost.
    • Description: reference images are generated from a text.
    • From photo: from your photos. In Characters and Locations you can also use the photos as they are, at no
  • Choose the number of references and the model (with its cost).
  • Project: leave it global or assign it to a project so it comes first in its pickers.

Each card

Rename, change project and regenerate references. Names are unique across the whole app.

Tips

  • Use short lowercase names without spaces (ana, corner_cafe, red_mug): they are the ones Script to Storyboard and the JSON use.
  • Only use photos of people who have given you permission.
