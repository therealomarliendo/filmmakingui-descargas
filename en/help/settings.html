Settings

  • Interface language: automatic (follows the browser), Spanish or English. It is saved on the computer and also applies from your phone. Prompts sent to the models stay in English.
  • License: status, product (Free trial, First month, Monthly or Annual), the day it expires, computers used, end of the updates period and last check. When 7 days or fewer are left, a notice with a Renew button appears. «Check now» (asks the service and the store again, for example after a refund) and «Deactivate this computer» (to move the license to another computer). The license checks itself every 7 days and works for 14 days offline.
  • About / Updates: the version you have, Check for updates and, if there is a new version, its news and the Update button. It downloads with a progress bar, its signature is checked (if it is not signed correctly it is not installed), a backup of the current program is made, it is installed and FilmmakingUI closes and reopens by itself. If the new version does not start, it goes back to the previous one by itself. Your projects, keys and settings are never touched. When you open the program, a discreet notice in the sidebar tells you if there is a new version. With a purchased license, updates are included while it lasts (30 days or 1 year). A manual license may have an updates period: versions released after it are not offered, and earlier ones keep working. If your program was installed with the installer, this section takes you to download the new installer instead of updating by itself.
  • Access from your phone: turns on your own secure address (HTTPS) to open FilmmakingUI from your phone, with a QR code. It always asks for your username and password. On the phone you can «Add to Home Screen» to have it as an app. Your computer must be on with FilmmakingUI open.
  • Data and output folders: where projects, library and results are stored. You can move them (files are copied; nothing is deleted from the old one).
  • Import data from a previous installation.
  • Ask for confirmation above: below that cost it generates without asking.
  • Reports: the address the «Report» button's notices go to (images and keys are never sent).
  • AI engine (text): «Claude through fal» (default, with your fal key) or «Anthropic direct» (advanced, with your own Anthropic key). Used by the Assistant, Script to Storyboard, the Explainer, the Tutorials, Dubbing and the AI Editor. If you choose Anthropic and no Anthropic key is saved, the app keeps using fal by itself.
  • API keys: your key for each provider, encrypted on this computer, with each one's status and where to get it.
  • Reset all tutorials.
