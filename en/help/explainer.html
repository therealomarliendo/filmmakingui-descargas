Explainer

Turns a topic into an explainer video: Claude writes the script by scenes, the voice and one image per scene are generated, and the video assembles itself in the editor, where you polish and export it. Every step is editable and repeatable.

Where it is

Sidebar → PRODUCTION → Explainer.

How to use it

  1. On the left, write what it is about, pick language, format (horizontal 16:9 or vertical 9:16), length and visual style, and press Create explainer.
  2. Script with AI: pick the model (Claude Sonnet 5.5 recommended), add instructions if you like and press Write the script. You see the cost before sending.
  3. Scenes: each one has its narration and its image description. Edit them, add or remove scenes. If you change a text, that voice or image is marked «out of date» and regenerated as a new version (nothing is overwritten).
  4. Voice, animation and music: pick the voice engine (ElevenLabs or MiniMax through fal, or ElevenLabs with your own key), the voice and the speed. Images move with a gentle zoom (free), stay still, or are animated with AI (one clip per scene, paid). Music is free: pick one of the 18 library tracks (epic, action, calm, corporate, emotional or fun; public domain CC0, from FreePD.com) with a preview, or the synthesized pad created on your computer. A track shorter than the video repeats to cover it. Add your own music from the editor, on the audio track.
  5. Generate: every button shows its cost. Do everything generates what is missing and assembles the video.
  6. Assemble in the editor (free): creates a montage with one clip per scene, the narration, subtitles and music. Press Open the montage to adjust effects and transitions and to export.

Rough costs (60 s video, 8 scenes)

  • Script with Claude Sonnet 5.5: a few cents.
  • Voices: ≈ $0.05 per 1,000 characters with ElevenLabs Turbo (≈ $0.03 the video).
  • Images: ≈ $0.04 each with the cheap model (≈ $0.31).
  • AI animation: the priciest part (≈ $0.05–0.25 per second depending on the model); the gentle zoom is free.

Notes

  • Keys (fal, Anthropic and, if you use it, ElevenLabs) are stored in Settings.
  • Generated files stay in your outputs; deleting an explainer does not delete them.
