Refund policy

Right of withdrawal for digital content

FilmmakingUI is digital content delivered immediately (the license key and the download). Under article 103.m) of the Spanish Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 and article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU, by buying you expressly agree that delivery starts immediately and acknowledge that, as a result, you lose the 14-day right of withdrawal. This acceptance is requested explicitly during checkout, before payment (a required checkbox or text in the Payhip store), and is confirmed in the purchase email.

Voluntary refund

We still offer a refund if, within 14 days of purchase:

  • the program does not work on your computer (meeting the published requirements) and support cannot fix it; or
  • you bought by mistake and have not activated the license on any computer.

How to request it

Use the “Request a refund” link at the bottom of every page of the website (request-refund.html): enter the purchase email, the order number and the reason (including withdrawal), check the summary and confirm it. When it is sent you will see a receipt with the date and time and the request number, which you can print; when possible we also email it to you. You can also write to soporte@filmmakingui.com with the order number. We reply within 14 days.

The refund is processed through the store (Payhip) to the same payment method. When refunded, the license is deactivated automatically (the app notices at its next check).

What is not refunded

The costs of AI generations: each provider charges them directly to your account (BYOK) and they do not go through us.

Legal guarantee

This policy does not limit the guarantees the law gives consumers if the digital content does not conform.
