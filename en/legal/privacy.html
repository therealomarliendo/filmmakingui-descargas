What we do NOT process

FilmmakingUI runs on your computer. Your projects, images, videos, prompts, characters and API keys stay on your computer: we do not receive or store them. When you generate, your computer sends the request directly to the AI provider you choose (fal, Higgsfield, ByteDance, Anthropic, ElevenLabs…) with your own key; that processing is governed by each provider's privacy policy, with whom you have your own account.

The app's «Report» button opens your own email program with the result's identifier, the model and the date: it never sends the image, the video, the prompt or any key, and nothing is sent until you send the email.

The website uses no cookies of any kind (tracking or otherwise) and no third-party analytics. To know how many people visit us, our own server (Cloudflare) counts how many times the home page and the download page are opened, how many times the installer or the portable version is downloaded (by version) and how many times each purchase button and "Try free for 7 days" are clicked. We only keep daily totals, the approximate country that Cloudflare infers from the connection and the kind of source (the ref of the link you came through, such as a store or a software directory, or whether you came from a search engine, a social network or another site): we do not store the IP, the browser, the referring page or any identifier, so these totals cannot identify or track anyone across visits. They are kept for 400 days. Legal basis: legitimate interest (knowing whether the website works and improving it). The purchase is made on Payhip's page, which has its own cookie policy.
