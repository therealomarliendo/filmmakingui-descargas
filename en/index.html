What it is

Assistant

Ask for what you want in the chat. It proposes a plan with the cost of each step, waits for your approval and does it; you see the storyboard and change a shot without leaving the conversation.

Film

Projects with scenes and shots. Paste a script and turn it into a storyboard faithful to what you ask for; generate, regenerate, animate and voice every shot.

Character library

Characters, locations and props reusable in every shot. Continuity depends on the model and on your references.

Editor

A built-in video editor: timeline, text, transitions and music, plus an AI editor that cuts for you.

Explainer and Tutorials

Narrated explainer videos and tutorials with a presenter, from the topic to the final cut.

Sequence

Long videos that look like a single take, through keyframes approved one by one.

Edit image and video

Image editing with references; for video, motion control, character replacement and lip-sync.

Marketing

What it looks like

Try the online demo

The demo runs in your browser, with nothing to download and nothing generated: it shows you what the app is like before you buy.

Bring your own key (BYOK)

FilmmakingUI does not resell credits. You connect your own accounts with the AI providers (fal.ai and, if you like, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Higgsfield or ByteDance) and each one charges you directly for what you generate, at its own price, with no markup. That spending is separate from the license.

Buttons that spend money show, when a price is known beforehand, an indicative estimate, ask for confirmation above the threshold you set in Settings and are disabled while they send. The real invoice is each provider's, and nothing is generated unless you ask.

Your keys are stored encrypted on your computer only. So are your projects, images and videos: they do not go through our servers.

You only need the fal.ai key: with it you generate images, video and voice, and Claude writes the texts («Claude through fal» engine, the default one). Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Higgsfield and ByteDance are optional. The first-launch welcome guides you step by step to create the fal key and test it before you start.

Requirements

  • Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel): in preparation. We will announce it on this page when it is available.
  • An internet connection to generate (generations run on the AI providers) and to check the license.
  • A fal.ai account with credit (BYOK). Optional: Higgsfield, ByteDance, Anthropic, ElevenLabs.

FAQ

What does «Early access» mean?

That FilmmakingUI can already be used in full (from script to finished video) and keeps growing: there are frequent updates, included in your license. Some bugs may remain; if you hit one, write to us and we will fix it in the next version.

How much does generating cost?

Whatever each provider charges, on top of the license. When a price is known beforehand, the app shows an indicative estimate before the action is sent (it does not always exist); for example, an image usually costs cents and a 5 s video, from a few cents to a couple of dollars depending on the model.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes: the app comes with a 7-day free trial with every feature, no card and no payment (one per computer). And the online demo needs no download.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every purchase is a one-time payment: the first month (€9.99), one month (€14.99) or the year (€99.99). It does not renew by itself and nothing else is charged: when it expires, the app offers you the Monthly or the Annual and your projects stay intact.

Can I repeat the first month?

No: the discounted first month (€9.99) is once per person and computer. After it you can go on with the Monthly (€14.99) whenever you need it or with the Annual (€99.99, it works out at €8.33/month).

What happens when the trial ends or the license expires?

The app shows the license screen with the plans (the First month only if you have never paid; the Monthly and the Annual, always). Your projects, images, videos and keys are not touched: when you activate a license everything is back.

What happens to my days if I change plan?

They are not lost: they are added. If you move from the First month or the Monthly to the Annual (or renew the Monthly) and activate the new key on the same computer, the new license starts counting when your remaining days run out. For example, with 10 days left on the First month, the Annual lasts 365 + 10 days.

Do I need internet?

To generate, yes. The license is checked every 7 days and works for 14 days offline.

Are my images uploaded to your servers?

No. Everything is stored on your computer; it only travels to the AI provider you choose when you generate.

Windows says «Windows protected your PC»

It is normal for new programs: «More info» → «Run anyway». If you have Smart App Control, use the portable version (see the download page).

What if it does not work for me?

See the refund policy: if it does not work on your computer and support cannot fix it, you get your money back.
